Projects

Schiavo Group, Queen Square Institute of Neurology

Supervised by Professor Giampietro Schiavo and Kai Sun | September 2026

Investigated the structural interactions of cytoplasmic dynein-1 heavy chain with LIC1/LIC2 under conditions of serine phosphorylation and G-domain overexpression using Co-IP and Western Blot competitive binding assays.

Fitzgerald’s Group, Early Cancer Institute, University of Cambridge

Supervised by Professor Rebecca Fitzgerald and Dr. Ahsen Ustaoglu | July 2026 - August 2026

Evaluated non-endoscopic capsule sponge assays and liquid biopsies for the early detection of oesophageal adenocarcinoma. Performed SIMOA and bioinformatic methylation analyses (EM-Seq vs. 5-Base) to identify and validate candidate protein (FABP2 and REG4) and epigenetic biomarkers.

Moss Group, UCL

Supervised by Dr. Guy Moss and Dr. Thomas Blacker | June 2026 - September 2026

Investigated gap-junctional coupling between airway epithelial cells and how CFTR-expressing cells may electrically support their non-expressing neighbours. Built a resistor-network computational model in R to fit published datasets and conducted FLIM using a genetically encoded voltage indicator (JEDI-2P) in HEK293T cells to measure membrane potential.

Sihra, UCL

Supervised by Professor Talvinder Sihra | September 2025 - June 2026

Analysed 82 peer reviewed studies, writing a 5000-word review on Phosphorylation Dependent Modulation of Presynaptic Plasticity, characterising a theoretical presynaptic multi-phosphocode, mapping how the temporal balance between competing kinase cascades encodes the direction and magnitude of synaptic plasticity.

Centre for Cardiovascular and Metabolic Neuroscience (CCMN), UCL

Supervised by Professor Alexander Gourine and Dr. Alice Braga | January 2025 - April 2025

Explored how astrocytic A2B receptor activation regulates brain energy metabolism and lactate release to support synaptic plasticity. Performed cryostat sectioning and immunostaining on murine brains to evaluate metabolic signalling pathways linked to cognitive health and neurodegeneration.
